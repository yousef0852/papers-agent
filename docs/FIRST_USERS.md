# AI Mind — First Users Plan

This is a plan, not code. The goal: get your **first 10 people** to try AI Mind, and learn whether they come back. Small and personal beats a big public launch right now.

Live link to share: **https://papers-agent.vercel.app**

---

## The goal (keep it tiny)

- Get **10 people** to open it and send at least one message to the tutor.
- Watch a few of them actually use it.
- See if **any come back a second time within a week.**

That's the whole game. Don't worry about hundreds of users yet.

---

## Step 1 — Make your list (15 minutes)

Write down real names. Aim for ~15 (some won't reply). Good candidates:

- Friends/classmates/coworkers who read about AI or ML
- People you've talked AI with online (X DMs, Discord, etc.)
- Anyone learning AI from scratch (your exact target user)

Put them in a simple list with a "sent?" and "tried it?" column — even a notes app is fine:

| Name | Where (DM / X / in person) | Sent? | Tried it? | Came back? |
|------|----------------------------|-------|-----------|------------|
| ...  |                            |       |           |            |

---

## Step 2 — Message the people you know (do this first)

Send a **personal, casual** message — not an announcement. One at a time. Here's a template; tweak per person:

> Hey [name] — I built a little thing I think you'd actually like. It's a notebook for learning the history of AI: you chat with a tutor and it draws a map of the papers and ideas as you go. I'm trying to figure out if it's actually useful or just a toy. Could you try it for 5 min and tell me honestly what you think? https://papers-agent.vercel.app

If they reply positively, ask the gold question:

> Would you be up for a quick 15-min call while you use it? I just want to watch where it confuses you.

**Watching 3–5 people use it live is the single most valuable thing you can do this week.** You'll learn more in 15 minutes of watching than from 100 analytics charts.

---

## Step 3 — One post on X (since that's where you are)

Keep it honest and human. Attach a short screen recording if you can (even 20 seconds of the map growing from a chat).

> I built a notebook for learning the history of AI.
>
> You chat with a tutor, and every paper and idea you discuss gets drawn onto your own timeline — a map that grows the more you read.
>
> It's early and free. I'd love feedback from anyone who reads AI papers: https://papers-agent.vercel.app

Then reply to your own post with: *"Built by two people. Tell me what's broken — that's why it's public."*

(There's a fuller launch kit with more posts in `docs/LAUNCH_KIT.md` for when you're ready to go wider. For now, the personal DMs matter most.)

---

## Step 4 — What to ask everyone who tries it

Three short questions (in DM or on the call):

1. What did you try first?
2. Where did it confuse you or feel slow?
3. Would you use this again next time you read about AI? Why / why not?

Write down the answers. Patterns across 5–10 people tell you what to fix next.

---

## What "success" looks like

- 10 people tried it
- You watched ~5 use it
- At least a couple **came back on their own** within a week

If people come back without you nudging them → you have something real, and we move to growing it.
If nobody comes back → that's not failure, it's the most useful signal there is, and we'll dig into *why* and fix the loop.

---

## What NOT to do right now

- Don't run paid ads.
- Don't build new features.
- Don't post everywhere at once.

Get 10 real people in, watch them, learn. That's the entire job this week.
